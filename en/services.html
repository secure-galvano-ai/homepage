What people ask first

The most common questions

How much of our time does this take? We have no one to spare.

On the most recent project, the customer team spent less than twenty hours in total over three months: twenty minutes of the morning meeting every month to month and a half, plus one person as the point of contact for one to two hours a week, checking whether the analyses are technically correct.

Do we have to get our data in order first?

No, as long as your readings are recorded in a database system — that is the basic prerequisite; Excel spreadsheets are not enough. The work is then done on the data you have today: logs from the line controller, sensor values, measurements, orders in the ERP system, much of it in backup files. Where something is missing later on, it is usually one or two additional exports from the controller. If the database is missing, we can help you choose the right partner and set up the recording so your data becomes ready for AI.

Does this interfere with our line controls?

No. The analysis uses what the controller already puts out; the controller is neither changed nor operated remotely, and no new sensors are needed. If an analysis keeps running, it sits on a server in your building, with no cloud; one hour of training is enough.

What happens to our data?

For the analyses you provide only the extract you release — handed over encrypted, once a mutual NDA is signed. The analysis runs on our own computers, and no AI service sees your readings. After the rollout, the tool runs in your building, with no cloud. Formulations are not needed, and what a tool is allowed to see is agreed in writing beforehand.

Which frameworks stand behind this

Does this replace our skilled staff?

No. Crusting, mechanical damage and the condition of a line show up when someone goes and looks, not in a series of numbers. The analysis takes the searching off your hands and tells you where to look first — the judgment stays with your process engineering and maintenance teams.

Whether this pays off for you becomes clear in half an hour

In the intro call you get an honest assessment — even if it is that it does not add up for your shop yet. Your contact is a master electroplater and materials physicist; you will not be sold any hardware.
