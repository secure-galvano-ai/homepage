What we look for

Four areas where plating shops have money on the table

Each area is assessed with your numbers, as an order of magnitude with a range; the calculation is spelled out in the report.

  • Cost of defects — recurring defects that, in day-to-day operations, only get fixed instead of understood.
  • Chemicals and disposal — chemicals added without anyone knowing whether it was needed.
  • Level of automation — maintenance planning, order entry and paper lists that tie up hours every week; the starting point is the AI Base Setup.
  • AI know-how in-house — a core team works with AI while formulations and readings stay in-house; the starting point is the AI Base Setup.

What typically comes out of it

  • The defect you cannot see on the part — reported before the batch ships.
  • Batch documentation in minutes — instead of hours of searching.
  • Early warnings for the on-call staff — by email or text message, with a plain-language note on what to check.

What people ask first

The most common questions

How much of our time does this take? We have no one to spare.

On the most recent project, the customer team spent less than twenty hours in total over three months: twenty minutes of the morning meeting every month to month and a half, plus one person as the point of contact for one to two hours a week, checking whether the analyses are technically correct.

Do we have to get our data in order first?

No. The work is done on the data you have today — logs from the line controller, sensor values, measurements, orders in the ERP system, much of it in backup files. Where something is missing later on, it is usually one or two additional exports from the controller.

Does this interfere with our line controls?

No. The analysis uses what the controller already puts out; the controller is neither changed nor operated remotely, and no new sensors are needed. If an analysis keeps running, it sits on a server in your building, with no cloud; one hour of training is enough.

What happens to our data?

For the analyses you provide only the extract you release — handed over encrypted, once a mutual NDA is signed. The analysis runs on our own computers, and no AI service sees your readings. For the AI Base Setup nothing is handed over to us; we work in your environment. Formulations are not needed, and what a tool is allowed to see is agreed in writing beforehand.

Which frameworks stand behind this

What if a probe is reading wrong?

A crusted or out-of-calibration probe does not report itself as faulty — it keeps delivering values, just the wrong ones. It is detected through measurements from different sources that normally move together: if they drift apart, the analysis names the most unusual measuring points of the day for a check — without an additional sensor.

Does this replace our skilled staff?

No. Crusting, mechanical damage and the condition of a line show up when someone goes and looks, not in a series of numbers. The analysis takes the searching off your hands and tells you where to look first — the judgment stays with your process engineering and maintenance teams.
