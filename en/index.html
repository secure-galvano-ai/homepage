The goal

Step in before the scrap is made.

One path, not a toolkit. It starts by finding the cause in the data you already have — and ends where you react before a deviation even occurs. The industry calls this Predictive Quality.

1 · Find the cause
In the data you already record. No intervention in production. 2 · See it live
Spot anomalies while the parts are still in your shop. 3 · React ahead of time
Ahead of the disturbance instead of behind it — before any scrap is made.

Your monitoring checks each reading on its own: temperature too high, alarm. That is exactly where it fails in the expensive cases. Because often every single value is in the green — and the combination is still wrong. The current a little lower, the bath a little cooler, the parts in a little longer. Unremarkable on their own, a defect together. That interplay is what we read.

“Not: something is wrong somewhere. But: at this point on the line, in this time window — look there first.”

What this means for you: How far it goes in your case depends on your data. The Site Assessment or the Initial Data Check shows what your data can support.

Older lines too

Your line doesn't have to be new.

Whether your values come from the controls, from the rectifier or by hand from the bath log — we analyze what is already being recorded today. Even on a line that has been running for twenty years, where every source writes its own format.

Where your data is processed

Out of several hundred measuring points and half a million signals, what remains is what matters — the few alerts with a real cause. The decision stays with your engineer: the AI points to the lead, the call is yours.

How we work with your data during the analyses

Step 1

Built on synthetic data

We build your analysis on a test plant with the same structure as your plant data, but without a single real value.

Step 2

Analyzed on our side

Your readings are analyzed exclusively on our own encrypted computers. No AI service sees them.

Step 3

Run at your site

The finished tool runs on your infrastructure, without an internet connection.

No third-party data center

The analysis runs on our own encrypted computers — no AI service, no third-party cloud. To build the tools, a software assistant only ever sees the structure of the data, that is table and column names of the control software, plus anonymized ratios. Readings, names and formulations stay here.

The results belong to you, with no ties to us. After the project ends, your data is fully deleted on request, with written confirmation. The audits and standards behind it

What data do you need — and what if our data isn't ideal?

What you already record: sensor logs, SCADA history, Excel records, lab reports, customer complaint reports. 3–12 months of history is a good start. Gaps, mixed formats and time offsets are the rule, not the exception — that gets cleaned up. Where your data can't answer a question, you get a clear statement saying so.

What if the cause can't be pinned down clearly?

Usually the main causes become clearly visible. In the difficult cases you still get a solid interim result: which hypotheses the data supports and which are ruled out, plus a proposal for the targeted additional measurement that settles the open question. Either way, you have more clarity than before.

We have several sites. How does that work?

It starts at one site — that one carries the analysis. At every additional site the method is already in place, so things move faster there. The analyses are billed per site or per defect pattern, ongoing support later per monitored line. You decide site by site.
